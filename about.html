ABOUT BEEP

A card game where every decision counts.

Beep is a browser-based card game that uses virtual credits for gameplay. Credits have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for real money. The game is designed for entertainment and programming practice.

Beep is a browser-based Blackjack game built around card values, player decisions, balance management, and fixed payouts. Start a round, receive your cards, decide whether to draw another card, and aim for a total of 21 without going over.

Every round starts with a fixed entry cost. Additional cards come with an extra charge, and a winning hand earns a fixed payout. Your balance changes as you play, so every decision affects your remaining funds.

How to Play

1. Start a Round

Select Start Game to begin a new round. The game deducts 200,000 credits from your balance and deals two cards.

You need enough credits to cover the entry cost before a round begins.

2. Check Your Cards

Your cards and their combined value appear on the game screen. Your goal is to reach a total of 21.

  • Below 21: Your hand is still active.
  • Exactly 21: You win the round.
  • Above 21: You lose the round.

3. Draw Another Card

Select Draw New Card when you want another card. Each additional draw costs 100,000 credits.

Every new card changes your total. Consider your current sum before drawing again.

4. Collect Your Payout

A winning hand earns a fixed payout of 2,100,000 credits. The payout is added to your remaining balance.

A losing hand ends the round without a payout.

5. Start Another Round

Select New Game to clear the previous hand and prepare for another round. Your remaining balance carries forward.

Game Costs and Payouts

Action Amount
Start a round 200,000 credits
Draw an additional card 100,000 credits
Win a round 2,100,000 credits

Your balance must cover each charge before the corresponding action proceeds. If your balance falls below the required amount, the game prevents the action.

Understanding Your Balance

Your balance represents the credits available for gameplay.

Starting a round reduces your balance by 200,000 credits. Drawing another card reduces the balance by a further 100,000 credits per draw. Winning adds the fixed payout of 2,100,000 credits to your remaining balance.

Your final balance depends on your starting funds, the costs incurred, and the outcome of each round.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the objective of Beep?

Reach a card total of 21 without exceeding the limit.

How much does a round cost?

Each round costs 200,000 credits.

Does drawing another card cost extra?

Yes. Each additional card costs 100,000 credits.

What happens when I win?

The game adds 2,100,000 credits to your remaining balance.

What happens when I lose?

The round ends, and no winning payout is awarded.

What happens when I run out of credits?

You must have enough credits to cover the cost of your next action before proceeding.

Does my balance reset after every round?

No. Your remaining balance carries into the next round while the current game session is active.

Play Responsibly

Beep uses a custom payment and payout system. The game does not follow every standard Blackjack rule, and its fixed payout does not guarantee a profit.

If Beep later supports deposits, withdrawals, or wagers involving real money, publish clear payment terms, age restrictions, and responsible gambling information before launch.
